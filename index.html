This Side Up

Photo proof for the boxes you ship and the ones you open.

A buyer says it arrived broken, and the carrier wants the photos you were supposed to take before you handed the box over. This Side Up walks you through those shots, keeps each package's photos together, and counts down the days you have left to file. Export everything as a PDF or a ZIP.

Steep Notes

A week of loose thoughts, brewed into a few sentences.

Drop in pieces all week: a line from a meeting, a worry, a half-formed idea. At the brew time you pick, Steep turns them into short sentences, and you keep the few worth keeping.

About the name

Good tea is often better on the second steep.

The first pour wakes the leaves up. The second is where a lot of loose-leaf tea really opens. That's how we like to build: ship something small, listen to the people using it, then come back and make it better.
