Second Steep Labs is an independent software company in Decatur, Georgia. Every app we make starts with a problem we ran into ourselves. We build for iPhone first, with Android to follow.

Our apps

  • This Side Up

    On the App Store

    Photo proof for the boxes you ship and the ones you open.

    It walks you through the photos your carrier requires, keeps each package's photos together, and counts down the days you have left to file a claim. When a claim comes in, export everything as a PDF or a ZIP.

    App Store thissideup.app

  • Steep Notes

    Coming soon

    A week of loose thoughts, brewed into a few sentences.

    Drop in pieces all week: a line from a meeting, a worry, a half-formed idea. At the brew time you pick, Steep turns them into short sentences, and you keep the few worth keeping.

    steepnotes.app

  • Brainfogg

    In beta

    Email in. Schedule done.

    Forward a school email to Brainfogg, and it puts the event on your calendar: the date, the time, and what your kid has to bring.

About the name

A lot of good loose-leaf tea tastes better on the second steep, once the leaves have opened up. That's how we like to build: ship something small, listen to the people using it, then come back and make it better.
